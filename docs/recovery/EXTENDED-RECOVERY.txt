Extended MPU4 recovery — 14 October 2026

This sustained pass combined native recovery, threaded-script decoding, long physical-input campaigns, isolated DIP experiments and repeated-run checks. Saved Ghidra annotations and supporting listings are complementary: the scripts are not ordinary 6809 functions, and decompiled pseudocode is not original source.

Saved project
477 native functions have display names; 18 retain explicit hypothesis names. A display name does not establish a complete semantic understanding: older init_step/address-based names and shared tails remain.36 functions were newly defined in three native structural passes; 24 previously missing VM-target/extra entrypoints and 14 service/helper entries were reviewed, with overlapping entries retained as aliases. Inline input, alpha, lamp and digit payloads were skipped rather than decoded as instructions.
526 threaded bodies indexed; 210 purpose names; 316 broader purposes unresolved; 411 bodies have at least some observed execution. Observation does not establish coverage of every branch.
9295 typed VM token sites and 1153 headers independently verified with zero missing definitions.178 parameterized words named. Four opaque static sites remain, behind disabled retail diagnostic guards; raw words and uncertainty are retained.

Recovered behavior and data
- Super Hold choices now use all three hold buttons and Start. Long campaigns continued beyond the formerly stalled feature and reached Adders & Ladders, Back3, Advance, Boost, Rattler, Roll Even and Up N Over.
- Wider cash-feature tracing and bounded dispatch-table recovery added Loot Shoot, Nice Dice, Cash Climber, Money Trap, Pick A Win, Skill Cash, Cash Attack, Super Stepper and Cash Jackpot Restart associations. A static display/dispatch association is not a verified complete feature path.
- BOARD-MAP.txt and evidence/extended-pass/board-map.json list all 48 board square display/type/link records. The data and three feature-dispatch token tables are typed/referenced in Ghidra.
- Weighted random selection 9A1C recovered: a 0..99 draw is reduced by successive byte weights until underflow. 17 traced selections checked independently, zero mismatches. Reviewed tables sum 100.
- VM PICK consumes a 16-bit index and replaces it with the indexed remaining stack cell.98 observations verified. Rotate right 724 observations and rotate left 52 observations verified, all with zero mismatches. The double-to-cell reduction helper 7813 is static recovery only.
- Target percentage selection reads key/DIP fields, defaults to 80, and converts P to 10 * P internally.70 and 98 cases reproduced the derived offsets. This is the configured target, not measured long-run payout.
- The100P locked alarm is a held physical coin-switch alarm. Native code at 4581/4586 shows counter 0018 passing0x50, negating 0x51 to 0xAF and raisingSWI withB = 0x9F. A 2 s pulse reproduced it; 50 ms did not; isolated coin-alarm inhibit suppressed it. No ROM or emulator defect is asserted.
- Attract on/off branches exercised under isolated settings. Existing MAME configuration/NVRAM remain preserved.
- Service/test menu and nested helpers recovered statically, including switch/lamp feedback, manual reel steps, sound, meter/output pulses, serial-link test and fourth-reel diagnostics. Physical service inputs are logged correctly, but the tested sequences have not yet entered the menu; runtime verification remains open. A 600s physical-input experiment held Test for 540s but never reached the menu-entry check; this is a caller/scheduling reachability issue still under investigation. Test 7 is a serial-link test, not a DIP test.

Reproducibility
13 discovery/replay pairs match exactly in state.csv, feature-state.csv, debug.log, ram-final.bin and input-levels.csv. Five DIP pairs additionally match dip-levels.csv and dip-settings.txt. The two longest pairs each cover 1800 virtual seconds; other runs use dedicated short fixtures and wider selective VM logging.
Fixtures, copied controllers/debugger commands, resolved configuration, launch arguments, absent initial NVRAM and hashes are under runs/extended-*. Sparse traces contribute observed sites only; they are excluded from consecutive-dispatch/branch validation. The accumulated full-dispatch validator checked6022535 continuations with zero mismatches and zero observed token mismatches.

Remaining work and practical limits
316 scripts still lack a broader purpose name. Many compose already understood stack, state, lamp and award helpers; naming them convincingly needs branch-specific game evidence. Cash feature paths and compensator state remain only partly verified. Physical payout/serial peripherals cannot be established from these MAME runs alone. Disabled diagnostic hooks should not be treated as live retail behavior.
Ghidra still reports decompiler warnings around some interrupt/indirect paths and inline-control code. Default prototypes omit register inputs and condition-code outputs; use assembly and trace evidence for decisions. Every newly recovered entry was checked for instruction length and payload boundaries; this is not a claim that every ROM byte is correctly decompiled.
